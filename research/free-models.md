# Free models, free tiers and what free costs

Read 2026-11-20. Pages were read through a summarising fetch tool, so quotes are tool-reported: re-check the wording before relying on it. Nothing here was run. Used by `zones/agents-and-models/free-is-not-free.qmd`.

## 1. The curated list (secondary source)
- https://github.com/12britz/awesome-free-models : README and file list read with `gh api` on 2026-11-20. Last push 2026-11-14, about 2,660 stars, CC0 licence file (the GitHub API reports `NOASSERTION`). 21 sections, "55 models, 250 tools". (a) Sections include open-weight models, free API providers, routers, local inference tools, coding assistants. (b) The maintainer's own banner lists corrections from one pass on 2026-11-14: ZeroLimitAI free tier is a 3-day trial only; AI21 trial is $10 for 7 days; Cerebras free tier replaced by a $5 30-day trial that needs a payment method; Chutes.ai free tier ended; Nebius free trial suspended 2026-07-13; SambaNova free plan tightened. (c) It says OpenRouter's free lineup "churns constantly". (d) It lists resellers claiming free access to closed frontier models (for example FreeAIapikey). NOT CONFIRMED: who pays for those, where prompts go, and whether the access is authorised by the model's owner. We opened none of them.
- Not checked: the list's `docs/` folder, CONTRIBUTING.md, and any entry's own terms.

## 2. Provider pages (primary sources)
- https://openrouter.ai/docs/faq and https://openrouter.ai/docs/api-reference/limits : (a) Free models are `:free` variants; "All new users receive a small free allowance"; 50 requests per day, or 1000 per day with at least 10 credits purchased; 20 requests per minute. (b) FAQ lists card, AliPay and USDC for adding credits; does not say a card is required for free models; a negative balance can block free models. (c) "Prompt and completion are not logged by default" by OpenRouter; provider retention varies and providers that log are not routed to unless a toggle is on. (d) Free-model change policy not stated; credits may expire after a year.
- https://aistudio.google.com/docs/pricing (redirected from ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing; truncated, partly a model-extracted summary) : (a) A Free column exists, with limits such as 5,000 free search requests per month for grounding; free-tier request limits for text models were NOT read. (d) "Google may make changes to this pricing from time to time."
- https://aistudio.google.com/docs/terms (redirected from ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) : (c) For Unpaid Services Google "uses the content you submit ... to provide, improve, and develop Google products", and human reviewers "may read, annotate, and process" input. For Paid Services Google "doesn't use your prompts ... to improve our products" but logs for a limited period. Access counts as a Paid Service when the account is linked to an active Cloud Billing account.
- https://console.groq.com/docs/rate-limits and https://console.groq.com/docs/your-data : (a) Separate "Free Plan Limits" and "Developer Plan Limits" tabs exist, but the rendered table did not show which column was which, so NO free-plan numbers were read. (c) "By default, Groq does not retain customer data for inference requests"; logs may be kept "up to 30 days" for reliability or abuse; usage metadata is always retained.
- https://opencode.ai/docs/zen/ : (a) Free models such as Big Pickle and Nemotron are mostly "free on OpenCode for a limited time"; one lists 100 requests per day. (c) Differs per model: one "does not use your data for model training and retains it for 30 days", another "retains your data and may use it for model training", others "zero-retention". (d) Some free models are already deprecated or scheduled to be.
- https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/pricing/ : (a) "10,000 Neurons per day at no charge" on Workers Free; resets daily at 00:00 UTC; past the limit "further operations will fail with an error". (b) Some models "require a paid billing method". (c) Data use not stated on this page.
- https://mistral.ai/pricing and https://help.mistral.ai/en/articles/347617-do-you-use-my-user-data-to-train-your-models : see `research/mistral.md`. Free Mistral Studio mode may use data for training. A free API tier is NOT CONFIRMED on the pricing page.
